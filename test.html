🎮 How to Test in This Playground

  • Surprise Gifts: Click Present Box. Watch the kitten bring you a wiggling gift box! Click the gift box directly on the kitten to unwrap a surprise with sparkle bursts.
  • Toys: Click Yarn, Butterfly, or Heart. Watch the kitten swat the rolling yarn ball, watch the butterfly flutter, or pop the heart into sparkles.
  • Edge Peekaboo: Click Edge Peekaboo. The kitten will walk to the screen border, duck behind it, and playfully peek back out!
  • Direct Kitten Interactions:
    • Single Click the kitten directly to open the care menu (Pet, Feed, Play, Sleep, Rename, Hide).
    • Double Click the kitten for quick-pet hearts.
    • Drag & Drop the kitten anywhere on your screen.
